### LinkedIn / Telegram / website (attach the 16:9 banner)

**How Velum works: four steps, full custody.**

Velum is a trading interface for Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain. It offers spot and perpetual trading without asking
users to hand over their keys or their funds.

1. **Connect a self-custody wallet.** Any wallet on Robinhood Chain. No registration, and Velum never takes custody of
   keys or assets.
2. **Trade spot around the clock.** Stock Tokens such as TSLA, NVDA and AAPL trade 24/7 through Uniswap liquidity, with
   a minimum output on every swap and settlement directly to the wallet.
3. **Open leveraged positions.** Long or short up to 5x with isolated margin, from the user's own perpetuals account.
   Every order is reviewed before signing: size, margin, price limit and estimated liquidation price.
4. **Private execution (in development).** Encrypted balances and positions, verified by zero-knowledge proofs, so
   trading strategies stay confidential.

Ten markets are live: TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, META, AMZN, MSFT, COIN, PLTR, AMD and NFLX.

Follow @velumtrade on X for product updates.

*Velum is an independent project on Robinhood Chain and is not affiliated with Robinhood or Uniswap. Leveraged trading
involves significant risk, including the loss of margin. This is not investment advice.*
