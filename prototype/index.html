import { openAccount, proveTrade } from "@velum/sdk";

// One wallet signature derives keys on the client.

// The proof shows the trade is valid without revealing it.

How it works

From wallet to private position in four steps.

Step 1

Shield

Move USDG or stock tokens from your wallet into your private balance. This deposit is public.

Step 2

Prove

Pick a trade. Your browser builds a zero-knowledge proof that it follows the rules.

Step 3

Relay

A relayer submits the proof and pays gas, so your wallet never shows up on the trade.

Step 4

Settle

The contract checks the proof and updates your encrypted notes. Only you can read the result.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this real money?

No. Velum runs on Robinhood Chain testnet. Test tokens have no value, so you can learn how private trading works without risk.

Can I go long and short?

Yes. Perps let you go long or short on each stock with up to 5× leverage. A maker takes the other side, and you can close on your own at any time.

Who can see my trades?

You can. The maker on the other side sees that one position, against a one-time address. Two risk services can read what they need to liquidate. The public only sees that a position changed.

Is it anonymous?

No. Moving tokens in and out is public. If you want deposits harder to link, vary amounts and timing and withdraw to a fresh wallet.

Can I lose more than my margin?

No. Each position has its own margin and losses stop there. You can lose all of it, so start small.

What if Velum goes offline?

Your free balance is yours. You can unshield with your own wallet as long as the chain runs, and a forced exit settles open positions after 7 days.
