## Wording rules (keep every post true)

- Say **Stock Tokens**, never "tokenized stocks".
- **Live now:** spot (real Uniswap swaps from your wallet, 24/7) and perps (real orders, up to 5x, from your own
  Hyperliquid account). **Not live yet:** the privacy layer. Always call it "next" or "coming", never "live".
- Velum never holds keys or funds: true for both spot and perps today.
- No logos of Robinhood, Uniswap or Hyperliquid, and nothing that suggests a partnership. Naming them as venues is fine.
- Anything about perps carries a risk line (leverage, liquidation).
- "Velum adds no fee" is true only while the builder fee is off (`NEXT_PUBLIC_HL_BUILDER_FEE` empty). Drop the line if
  you turn the fee on.

Standard disclaimer (put it on banners and long posts):
> Independent project on Robinhood Chain. Not affiliated with Robinhood, Uniswap or Hyperliquid. Trading carries risk;
> leverage can lose your margin.
