**"Who holds my funds? Is it safe?"**
<!-- post -->
Only you. Spot trades settle to your wallet through Uniswap, and perps run from your own Hyperliquid account. Velum never holds funds or keys. Market and smart-contract risk still apply, so trade only what you can afford to lose.
<!-- /post -->

**"Is it private already?"**
<!-- post -->
Not yet. Spot and perps are live and public today. The privacy layer (encrypted balances and positions, checked by zero-knowledge proofs) is next. We'll announce it here first.
<!-- /post -->

**"Are you Robinhood?"**
<!-- post -->
No. Velum is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain and is not affiliated with Robinhood.
<!-- /post -->

**"How do I deposit for perps?"**
<!-- post -->
Deposit USDC on Hyperliquid (app.hyperliquid.xyz) with the same wallet, then open the Perps tab on Velum. One wallet signature enables trading for 30 days. It can't withdraw your funds.
<!-- /post -->

**"Fees?"** (only while the builder fee is off)
<!-- post -->
Velum adds none. Spot: the Uniswap pool fee plus gas. Perps: Hyperliquid's trading fee plus funding.
<!-- /post -->
