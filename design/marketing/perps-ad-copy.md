# "Perps are open": ad copy

Copy for `video/velum-perps-are-open-1080p.mp4` (0:44, 1080p, with music; poster `video/velum-perps-are-open-poster.jpg`).
Every X post below fits 280 characters (checked by `node design/marketing/check-x-posts.cjs
design/marketing/perps-ad-copy.md`; a link counts as 23).

House rules: the venue is not named in marketing; perps are real leveraged trades from the user's own account, funded
from Robinhood Chain (ETH or USDG), Ethereum (ETH) or Arbitrum (USDC); up to 5×, isolated margin; no promised returns
and a word on risk; Velum is independent and not affiliated with Robinhood. The video is labelled "product demo · test
account" and its price move "illustrative", so the copy never presents the P&L on screen as a real result.
