# How Velum works: professional copy

The formal version of the "How Velum works" copy, for the website, decks, partners, press and the `-pro` banners
(`velum-how-it-works-pro-1600x900.png`, `velum-how-it-works-pro-1080x1350.png`, `velum-x-header-pro-1500x500.png`,
source `banners-pro.html`). Tone: precise, calm and factual, with no slang, no emoji and no promises about
returns. The casual version stays in `how-velum-works-copy.md`.

What is live and what is not, stated the same way everywhere: spot and perpetuals are **live**; private execution is
**in development**.

---

## English

**Eyebrow:** How Velum works

**Headline:** Trade Stock Tokens. Keep full custody.

Alternatives:
- Stock Token trading, without a custodian.
- Spot and perpetuals for Stock Tokens, from accounts you control.
- Onchain access to Stock Tokens, on your own terms.

**Sub-headline:** Spot and perpetual trading for Stock Tokens, executed from accounts you control. No registration. No
custodian.

**Steps**

| # | Title | Banner line | Extended (website, deck) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Connect a self-custody wallet | Use any wallet on Robinhood Chain. Velum never takes custody of your keys or assets. | Velum works with any wallet on Robinhood Chain. There is no account to register and no deposit into Velum: your keys and assets stay under your control throughout. |
| 02 | Trade spot around the clock (Live) | Buy and sell Stock Tokens 24/7 through Uniswap liquidity, settled directly to your wallet. | Spot orders execute against curated Uniswap pools on Robinhood Chain, seven days a week. Each trade carries a minimum output set by your slippage limit, and the tokens settle directly to your wallet. |
| 03 | Open leveraged positions (Live) | Long or short up to 5x with isolated margin on Hyperliquid order books, from your own account. | Perpetual positions are placed on Hyperliquid's stock perpetual markets from your own Hyperliquid account, with isolated margin and leverage of up to 5x. Every order is an immediate limit order bounded by your price limit. |
| 04 | Private execution (In development) | Encrypted balances and positions, verified by zero-knowledge proofs. | Velum's privacy layer is designed to keep balances, positions and P&L in encrypted notes that only your device can read, with zero-knowledge proofs allowing the chain to verify each trade without seeing it. |

**Proof points** (short lines for a website strip or a deck slide)
- Self-custody: no account, no deposit into Velum.
- 10 Stock Token markets: TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, META, AMZN, MSFT, COIN, PLTR, AMD, NFLX.
- Spot available 24/7, including weekends.
- Perpetuals up to 5x, isolated margin.
- Full review before signing: size, margin, price limit, estimated liquidation price and fees.
